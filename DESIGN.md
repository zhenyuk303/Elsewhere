# Design decisions

## Login carousel photography

- Keep the landmark unobstructed. Reject trees, poles, signs, or foreground objects covering the tower or its base. The first-round Shaanxi candidate 02 was rejected because trees obstructed the pagoda.
- Use deliberate architectural or landscape photography: balanced framing, controlled perspective, clean backgrounds, clear light, and sufficient detail. Reject casual street snapshots even when the landmark is recognizable; Shaanxi candidates 01 and 03 were rejected for this reason.
- Review the photograph in the actual centered SVG crop and rotating color-window animation. Keep the landmark readable in grayscale and make its color reveal meaningful.

- The approved replacement for Shaanxi is second-round candidate 02, the elevated blue-hour pagoda view; preserve its reviewed square framing.

- For Orange Isle, preserve the full head and neck inside the settled rotating color window, with space above the hair. Review desktop and mobile widths. The approved second-round candidate 02 emphasizes the golden-lit face; its wide shoulder platform extends outside the color window.

- Orange Isle candidates should give the sculpture visual weight against a simple sky background and strong light. Reject distant aerial views dominated by plazas, crowds, buildings, or miscellaneous foreground detail, even when the sculpture fits the window.
